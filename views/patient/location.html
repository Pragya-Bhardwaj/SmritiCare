📡 Accurate Location

🔋 Battery Efficient

Smart location tracking that minimizes battery drain by updating only when you move.

🛡️ Privacy Protected

Only your linked caregiver can see your location. Your data is encrypted and secure.

⏸️ Easy Control

You can pause location sharing anytime. It resumes automatically when the app is opened.

ℹ️ Note: Location sharing runs in the background on all pages. The banner at the top of your screen will remind you when it's active. Make sure to keep location permission enabled in your phone's settings for best results.

🛡️ Your Privacy & Security

How your location is protected:

  • Encrypted transmission: All location data is sent over secure HTTPS connections
  • Limited access: Only your linked caregiver can view your location
  • Data retention: Location history is automatically deleted after 7 days
  • Your control: You can pause sharing or stop it completely anytime
  • Accurate location: Only precise GPS coordinates are stored, not address details

How your caregiver uses this:

  • To ensure your safety and well-being
  • To quickly find you in case of emergency
  • To understand your daily routine and movement patterns
  • To provide better care and support

Question? Contact your caregiver if you have any concerns about location sharing.
